How to Play

Game Rules:

  • Two teams (Team A and Team B) play alternately.
  • There are 3 rounds with 5 questions each.
  • Each question has 7 possible answers.
  • Try to guess the most popular answers from a kid survey!
  • Each team has 3 tries per question.
  • If all 3 guesses are wrong, the other team gets a chance to steal the points!

Scoring:

  • Round 1: Most popular answer = Most points (Total: 100 points)
  • Round 2: Same as Round 1, but points are doubled! (Total: 200 points)
  • Round 3: Reverse scoring - least popular answer = most points! (Total: 300 points)
